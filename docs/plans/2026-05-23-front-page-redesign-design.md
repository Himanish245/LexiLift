# Front Page Redesign

## Goal
Redesign the LexiLift landing page to match the provided mockup visual design. The page must be driven by Sanity CMS schema data, with the new design's copy and assets integrated as built-in editorial fallbacks so that the site looks identical to the screenshot out-of-the-box.

## Styling & Theme
*   **Background**: Soft warm cream (`#fbf9f5` / Tailwind `bg-surface`).
*   **Headings**: Editorial serif typography (`font-serif` using the Google Font *Literata*).
*   **Body & Actions**: Clean modern sans-serif (`font-sans` using *Plus Jakarta Sans*).
*   **Colors**: Neutral charcoal text (`#1b1c1a`), Forest Green primary elements (`#42634f`), warm rust secondary accents (`#8c4e37`).

## Component Details

### 1. Navbar
*   **Logo**: Text `LexiLift` in editorial green font.
*   **Links**: Platform, Resources, Pricing, Company. Active style with solid underline for "Platform".
*   **Right Side**: "Login" link and "Get Started" dark green pill button.

### 2. HeroSection
*   Tagline pill: "Intelligent Knowledge Hub" in light-gray rounded badge.
*   Headline: Left-aligned large serif title: "Transform Your Team's Knowledge into Shared Wisdom."
*   Body: Paragraph describing the "living digital sanctuary".
*   CTAs: Solid green pill "Start Free Trial" and outlined/play-icon "Watch Story".
*   Visual (Right): Olive-green background card framing the team meeting image (`public/team_meeting.png`), with an overlapping "Insight Generated" quote widget.

### 3. Trusted Logos
*   Subtle uppercase caption: "TRUSTED BY VISIONARY TEAMS AT".
*   Row of 5 neutral gray rounded blocks.

### 4. FeaturesGrid ("Cultivating Clarity")

    3.  Card 3 (Natural Inquiry - Narrow): White card with top badge & text.
    4.  Card 4 (Shared Evolution - Wide): Forest green background, white text, right-aligned "Explore Collaborative Hub" white button.

### 5. CTASection
*   Centered banner with subtle orange-cream top-right gradient.
*   Title: "Ready to cultivate your team's wisdom?".

### 6. Footer
